# Cabrillo Weather 🌾

A streamlined mobile dashboard (Progressive Web App) for the **Cabrillo Weather Station** in Half Moon Bay, CA (operated by San Mateo Resource Conservation District & Western Weather Group).

Built specifically for smartphones and outdoor farm conditions, featuring:
* **Glanceable Current Conditions:** Large, high-contrast numbers for Temperature, Dew Point, Humidity, and Today's High/Low.
* **Wind & Spray Conditions:** Live wind speed, peak gusts, and compass direction.
* **Precipitation Tracker:** Rain today, this month, and total season accumulation.
* **Agronomy Metrics:** Evapotranspiration (Daily ETo), Solar Radiation, and Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD).
* **Forecast vs. Actual Comparison:** Side-by-side comparison of official regional weather predictions vs. actual station microclimate measurements (with historical variance).
* **24h & 7d Trend Charts:** 15-minute resolution temperature, dew point, and wind curves.
* **Station Battery Indicator:** Live solar battery voltage monitoring.
* **1-Tap Mobile Install:** Installable as a standalone app on iOS and Android with custom app icon and full-screen launch.

## Deploying for Free (5 Minutes with GitHub)

### Option A: Deploy to Vercel (Recommended)
1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and click **"Sign Up"** $\rightarrow$ **"Continue with GitHub"**.
2. Click **"Add New..."** $\rightarrow$ **"Project"**.
3. Import your `cabrillo-weather` GitHub repository.
4. Click **"Deploy"**.
5. Within 30 seconds, Vercel will give you a live HTTPS link (e.g. `cabrillo-weather.vercel.app`).

### Option B: Deploy to Netlify
1. Go to [netlify.com](https://netlify.com) and click **"Log In"** $\rightarrow$ **"GitHub"**.
2. Click **"Add new site"** $\rightarrow$ **"Import an existing project"** $\rightarrow$ select your `cabrillo-weather` repository.
3. Click **"Deploy Cabrillo Weather"**.
4. You get an instant live link (e.g. `cabrillo-weather.netlify.app`).

## How to Install the App on a Phone

Once your link is live:
* **On iPhone:** Open the link in **Safari** $\rightarrow$ Tap the **Share icon** (square with arrow up) $\rightarrow$ Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
* **On Android:** Open the link in **Chrome** $\rightarrow$ Tap the **"Install"** banner at the top (or tap the 3 dots $\rightarrow$ **"Add to Home screen"**).
